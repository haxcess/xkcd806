The curve traced on the dial is the analemma for whatever clock time is shown — every date in the current year, all observed at that same moment. Drag the slider to see the figure reshape at other times of day; the shape is narrowest and most symmetric near local solar noon (around 12:35 PM in Calgary) and stretches out — sometimes dipping below the horizon for parts of the year — earlier or later in the day. Press Live to return to the real-time position.
